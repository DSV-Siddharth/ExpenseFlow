# ExpenseFlow

ExpenseFlow is a full-stack web application for managing and splitting shared household or roommate expenses.

## Problem

When multiple people share expenses, it becomes difficult to keep track of who paid for what and who owes whom.

ExpenseFlow solves this by tracking shared expenses, calculating individual balances, and simplifying repayments.
